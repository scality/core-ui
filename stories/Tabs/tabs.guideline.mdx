# Tabs

Tabs split one subject into views the user switches between, keeping the page and
its context. Each `Tab` owns a route, so a view is linkable and survives a reload.

<Canvas of={TabsStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

## How much inset the content gets

A tab insets its content on all four sides. Content that draws to the frame
instead — a table, a list, anything painting its own background — asks for
`withoutPadding`.

```tsx
<Tab path="/users" label="Users" withoutPadding>
  <UserTable />
</Tab>
```

Nothing else is a call-site decision. In particular, a `Form` with
`layout={{ kind: 'tab' }}` reserves a gutter for its own scrollbar at its right
edge, and takes the tab's inset back on that side by itself, at any depth, so
the scrollbar sits at the tab's frame.

A wrapper between the tab and such a form has to leave its own right edge alone
— padding there pushes the form's gutter back inward, `overflow: hidden` clips
the part reaching out — and has to leave the form the full width, which a
row-direction flex container does not. The symptom is the same either way: the
scrollbar sits away from the frame again. A wrapper that stacks vertically,
styles or conditionally renders its child is fine.

To drop the inset, use `withoutPadding`, not a CSS override on
`.sc-tabs-item-content`: the override leaves a form inside reaching a full inset
past the frame.
